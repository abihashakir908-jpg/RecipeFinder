Our Mission

Preserving Recipes Across Generations

Pakistani cuisine is a tapestry woven from centuries of Mughal grandeur, Persian influence, and regional traditions stretching from the mountain valleys of the north to the coastal plains of Sindh. Every recipe carries a story.

At Zaiqa, our mission is simple — to make authentic Pakistani recipes accessible to everyone: from a Lahori kid living abroad who misses Ammi's biryani, to a home cook discovering Pakistani food for the very first time.

The Journey

How Zaiqa Came to Life

The Idea

It started with a simple craving — Nihari on a cold Sunday. Unable to remember the exact recipe, we realized there was no single, comprehensive Pakistani recipe resource that felt premium and trustworthy.

The Research

We spent months collecting recipes from grandmothers in Karachi, dhaba owners in Peshawar, and family cookbooks across Punjab and Sindh — ensuring every dish is rooted in tradition.

The Build

Zaiqa was carefully designed from the ground up with features Pakistani food lovers actually need: scaling, cooking mode timers, meal planning, and smart shopping lists.

Zaiqa Today

With 60+ authentic recipes spanning 10 categories, Zaiqa is now a trusted companion in kitchens across the country and around the world — anywhere Pakistani food is cooked and cherished.
